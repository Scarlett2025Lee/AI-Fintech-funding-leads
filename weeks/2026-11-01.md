## How to Use Each Tag (Internship Context)

**`match` companies** → Cold-email pitching an *internship* in the tagged function (PM, GTM, or DA). The listed full-time role is not what you're applying for — it's proof the company is investing in that function right now. Open with a specific insight about their space or a 90-day deliverable you'd own, and name the function explicitly: *"I'd love to intern on the GTM side as you build out your partnerships motion."*

**`cold` companies** → Still worth an email, but you'll need to make the case for why an internship makes sense *now*, without a live signal. Lead with the funding momentum and a concrete problem you'd tackle: *"Seed round + YC backing = you're about to scale acquisition — I'd love to help design that growth experiment as an intern."*

## Suggested Cold-Email Angles

### 1. "I help AI infrastructure companies find their first enterprise customers"
General Compute ($100M Series A) and Hyperbolic ($30M Series A-1) are both GPU-compute platforms hitting the market at the same moment. A GTM or BD intern who understands the AI developer persona — and can articulate the difference between a price-per-GPU pitch and a latency-and-reliability pitch — stands out immediately. Lead with a specific channel: *"I'd focus the first 90 days on a referral loop through AI research labs, which are your highest-intent trial users."*

### 2. "I bring structured product thinking to regulated, workflow-heavy AI"
Avante (healthcare prior-auth, $30M) and Plato (research automation, $16M) both operate in spaces where the product is only as good as the workflow model it encodes. A PM intern candidate who has mapped a healthcare revenue-cycle or a research-lab approval process can open with that specificity: *"Prior-auth is a multi-party workflow — I'd anchor the first automation surface on the payer-response loop, where latency is most visible to the provider."*

### 3. "Series A = the GTM or product function you're staffing right now is mine to learn"
All four `match` companies just closed Series A rounds in the same week. Frame the internship as a chance to build the function from near-scratch alongside the first full-time hire: *"You're bringing on a GTM lead right now — I'd love to be the intern who runs the first market-mapping and competitor-signal work so that hire can move fast on day one."*
