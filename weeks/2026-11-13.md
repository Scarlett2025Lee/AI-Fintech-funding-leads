## How to Use Each Tag — Internship Context

**`match`** — The company is actively hiring in a PM, GTM, or DA-adjacent function. Cold-email lead with the angle: *"I saw [role] is open — I'd love to explore an internship in that function."* Tracks:
- **PM** — product manager / associate PM roles
- **GTM** — growth, marketing, BD, partnerships, sales ops, BDR, RevOps, AE
- **DA** — data analyst, BI, analytics engineer

**`cold`** — No confirmed PM/GTM/DA opening. Still worth emailing if the stage and fit are strong. Angle: *"I saw your recent funding — I'm interested in contributing to [relevant function] as an intern."*

> **Reminder:** These leads are for INTERNSHIP cold outreach only. Do not apply to full-time postings.

## Suggested Cold-Email Angles

### 1. "I turn AI spend into measurable business outcomes"
Larridin ($30M Series A) and geniosAI ($19.1M combined) are both in the business of making AI legible to enterprise decision-makers — Larridin tracks productivity impact, geniosAI makes financial AI analysis explainable and auditable. A GTM or PM intern who understands the CFO/CIO buying motion can open with a specific deliverable: *"Enterprise buyers approve AI tools faster when you can show attribution to a business metric — I'd spend 90 days building a customer-ROI narrative for your top 5 use cases."*

### 2. "I help fintech infrastructure companies build their first commercial motion"
Firmly ($15M, agentic commerce) and Alta ($47.5M, compliance blockchain) are both infrastructure plays backed by major fintech strategics (Mastercard + JPMorgan; Index Ventures + Airwallex). Both are at a stage where the product is real but the GTM narrative is still being shaped. Lead with a specific partnership or market-segment angle: *"The Mastercard Agent Connect integration gives you distribution — I'd map the top 500 merchants by AI-shopping readiness and build the outbound motion from there."*

### 3. "Voice AI for regulated industries needs a GTM intern who speaks the buyer's language"
Guava ($12.5M seed) has an Acrew Capital-backed CEO with deep fintech roots (former Marqeta CRO) and an open B2B marketing seat. Regulated-industry voice AI has a specific procurement cycle — compliance review, security review, pilot — that generic tech GTM experience doesn't address. Lead with proof of that domain knowledge: *"Insurance and healthcare voice agents have an SOC 2 + HIPAA procurement gate that shows up early — I'd own the first draft of Guava's compliance-readiness narrative for enterprise buyers."*
