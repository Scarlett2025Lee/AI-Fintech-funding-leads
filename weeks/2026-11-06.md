## How to Use Each Tag — Internship Context

**`match`** — The company is actively hiring in a PM, GTM, or DA-adjacent function. Cold-email lead with the angle: *"I saw [role] is open — I'd love to explore an internship in that function."* Tracks:
- **PM** — product manager / associate PM roles
- **GTM** — growth, marketing, BD, partnerships, sales ops, BDR, RevOps, AE
- **DA** — data analyst, BI, analytics engineer

**`cold`** — No confirmed PM/GTM/DA opening. Still worth emailing if the stage and fit are strong (e.g., Trajectory at Series A with Sequoia, or Lazarus with strategic insurance investors). Angle: *"I saw your recent funding — I'm interested in contributing to [relevant function] as an intern."*

> **Reminder:** These leads are for INTERNSHIP cold outreach only. Do not apply to full-time postings.
