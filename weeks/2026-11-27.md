## How to Use Each Tag — Internship Context

**`match`** — The company is actively hiring in a PM, GTM, or DA-adjacent function. Cold-email lead with the angle: *"I saw [role] is open — I'd love to explore an internship in that function."* Tracks:
- **PM** — product manager / associate PM roles
- **GTM** — growth, marketing, BD, partnerships, sales ops, BDR, RevOps, AE
- **DA** — data analyst, BI, analytics engineer

**`cold`** — No confirmed PM/GTM/DA opening. Still worth emailing if the stage and fit are strong. Angle: *"I saw your recent funding — I'm interested in contributing to [relevant function] as an intern."*

> **Reminder:** These leads are for INTERNSHIP cold outreach only. Do not apply to full-time postings.

## Suggested Cold-Email Angles

### 1. "I help seed-stage AI investing platforms build their first growth motion"
Supertake ($20M seed, TechCrunch Nov 23) is the standout company of the week — a USV-incubated, Adjacent-led platform that turns plain-language predictions into managed portfolios. The TechCrunch article names growth marketing as an explicit use of funds, which means this hire is imminent. A GTM intern who understands how retail investors discover and trust AI-managed products can open with a specific insight: *"The highest-activation users of an AI investing product aren't people who already trade — they're people who have opinions about markets but no mechanism to act on them. I'd design the first acquisition experiment that finds those users."*

### 2. "Privacy AI and AI accounting both live or die on trust — I help build that trust commercially"
Maple ($5.3M seed) and COUNT ($6.5M seed) are at opposite ends of the data-sensitivity stack — Maple protects the AI interaction layer, COUNT manages financial records — but both need operators who understand why their customers care about control and confidentiality. A PM or GTM intern with a privacy, fintech, or accounting-services background can lead with the buyer's anxiety: *"Accountants won't onboard their client data to a new platform unless the first question they can answer is 'what happens to my data.' I'd own COUNT's first trust narrative — the one-pager that answers that question before the demo."*

### 3. "Enterprise AI security is a function that every security team is about to need but doesn't have yet"
Metano ($12M seed) exited stealth on November 19 with a fully-available platform, which means it is actively looking for early enterprise design partners and needs a GTM motion quickly. A PM or GTM intern with enterprise sales, IT governance, or cybersecurity experience can open by naming a real AI-agent attack surface: *"Shadow AI agents — the ones deployed by individual employees without IT approval — are the biggest gap in every enterprise security posture right now. I'd spend 90 days building Metano's outbound motion around that exact discovery use case."*
