## Suggested Cold-Email Angles

### 1. "I help AI products find product-market fit in regulated industries"
A strong opener for Pennant, ScribeUp, Plinto, Marrow, and Nevis—all operating where compliance, audit trails, and institutional trust matter. Frame your PM/GTM experience around navigating procurement cycles, regulatory constraints, and the specific feedback loops (proxy votes, ERP integrations, RIA workflows) that drive adoption in this segment. Reference one real constraint they'll recognize: e.g., "proxy advisors require SOC 2 Type II before procurement goes live."

### 2. "I've worked at the intersection of AI tooling and financial infrastructure"
Ideal for Mural, Asymptote Labs, Ultra Security, and AuxoAI—companies selling to engineering and security buyers inside fintech/FSI enterprises. Position yourself as someone who can translate between the technical (what the AI agent actually does) and the commercial (why a risk officer or CFO approves the budget). Even one data or analytics project inside a bank or fintech makes this angle credible.

### 3. "Series A/B momentum = the GTM or data function you're building right now is mine to own"
Use this framing for Mural ($15M), ScribeUp ($13M), AuxoAI ($20.7M), or Nevis ($70M)—companies that just closed but haven't yet filled out their go-to-market or analytics teams. Offer to come in as a founding GTM or first data hire and reference a specific metric they'd want to move: e.g., "I'd focus on reducing your time-to-activation for the first 10 CU integrations" or "I'd instrument the Schwab advisor funnel from demo to active usage."
