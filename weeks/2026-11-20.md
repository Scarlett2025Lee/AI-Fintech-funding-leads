## How to Use Each Tag — Internship Context

**`match`** — The company is actively hiring in a PM, GTM, or DA-adjacent function. Cold-email lead with the angle: *"I saw [role] is open — I'd love to explore an internship in that function."* Tracks:
- **PM** — product manager / associate PM roles
- **GTM** — growth, marketing, BD, partnerships, sales ops, BDR, RevOps, AE
- **DA** — data analyst, BI, analytics engineer

**`cold`** — No confirmed PM/GTM/DA opening. Still worth emailing if the stage and fit are strong. Angle: *"I saw your recent funding — I'm interested in contributing to [relevant function] as an intern."*

> **Reminder:** These leads are for INTERNSHIP cold outreach only. Do not apply to full-time postings.

## Suggested Cold-Email Angles

### 1. "I help Series B AI platforms build the growth and analytics function that scales to $1B"
Linq ($90M, Series B) and LanceDB ($57.5M, Series B) both closed in the same week and are at the stage where the GTM or analytics infrastructure becomes what determines whether the round compounds. A GTM or DA intern can open with a specific 90-day deliverable: *"I'd spend the first 90 days mapping Linq's highest-intent enterprise accounts — the ones where AI agents in messaging can displace a Zoom + calendar stack — and building the activation funnel that proves it."* For LanceDB, the angle is different but equally concrete: *"I'd instrument the developer-to-enterprise conversion funnel — from first GitHub star to first paid seat — so the sales team has a lead-scoring model on day one."*

### 2. "I bring compliance fluency to AI products that live inside banks"
Arva AI ($20M Series A) and Agentcard ($8M seed) are both operating where every product decision runs through a compliance gate. A PM or GTM intern who has worked inside a bank, fintech, or regtech — or who can articulate why KYC accuracy matters to a BSA officer — opens the door that a generic pitch cannot. Lead with the buyer's constraint: *"AML teams approve new tooling through a 6-month vendor risk assessment — I'd spend 90 days building Arva's compliance-readiness narrative so that gate opens faster."*

### 3. "AI-native CRM and document intelligence are the two functions every B2B company needs to rebuild"
Dreamteam ($53M, Accel) and Unsiloed AI ($7.5M, Felicis) are at opposite ends of the stack — Dreamteam owns the sales motion layer, Unsiloed AI owns the document-extraction layer — but both need a GTM intern who understands the enterprise buyer persona. Lead with the customer insight: *"B2B sales teams are spending 60% of their time on tasks Dreamteam's AI teammates already handle — I'd design the outbound motion that gets you in front of that realization, before the buyer finds it themselves."* For Unsiloed AI: *"Financial-document parsing is the first thing a fintech AI pipeline breaks on at scale — I'd build the first vertical case study for the mortgage or lending use case that makes that point undeniable."*
