## Suggested Cold-Email Angles

### 1. "I bring structured thinking to the AI-driven CFO / finance office"
Hyphenate, Casap, and Sava all need operators who understand finance workflows deeply enough to define the automation boundary — where the AI acts vs. where a human reviews. A PM or DA candidate with FP&A, revenue-ops, or fintech experience can open with a specific workflow: *"Month-end reconciliation is still 80% manual at most growth-stage companies — that's where I'd anchor the first automation surface."* Referencing a real pain point (DSO, dispute-cycle time, subledger latency) makes the email land differently than a generic intro.

### 2. "I can grow a regulated consumer product from 250K users to 2.5M"
Nous is mid-expansion with a $17M raise, entering insurance and international markets. A GTM or product candidate who has scaled a consumer fintech in the UK or EU — especially around FCA registration, open banking, or household finance — has immediate credibility. Lead with a specific retention or conversion lever: *"Subscription-management products live or die on whether you surface an insight before the renewal date — I'd own that timing model."*

### 3. "I help Series B companies build the data layer that makes enterprise retention self-funding"
Both Casap ($56M, Series B) and Nevis ($70M, Series B) are at the stage where customer-facing analytics — dispute-resolution funnels, advisor-activity scorecards, fraud-model drift dashboards — become core to enterprise expansion. A DA candidate from B2B SaaS can pitch a specific 90-day deliverable: *"I'd stand up a real-time dispute-resolution view for your bank clients, tied directly to your chargeback-reversal SLA."*
